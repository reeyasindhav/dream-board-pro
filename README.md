Design Ideas: Soft motivational aesthetic, drag-and-drop board builder, goal milestone tracker, mood journal integration, inspiration quote cards. 

Problem Statement:

Many individuals struggle to translate their personal aspirations and long-term life visions into actionable daily progress. Traditional physical vision boards are static, difficult to update, and lack actionable tracking mechanisms, while most standard productivity applications focus strictly on rigid task management rather than creative visualisation and emotional well-being. This disconnect often leads to lost motivation, lack of clarity, and inconsistent personal growth tracking. Dreamboard addresses this gap by providing an intuitive, aesthetically engaging digital platform that merges creative visualisation with structured execution. Through interactive drag-and-drop vision boards, milestone tracking, mood journaling, and motivational content, the application bridges the divide between visual inspiration and measurable self-improvement in a single unified space.
